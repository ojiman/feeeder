# 0058 Unagi Reader defines its own requirements

- Status: valid (approved by the owner in chat on 2026-10-16).
- Date: 2026-10-16
- Decider: the owner asked that the product's purpose, design reasons and acceptance criteria stand on their own.
- Changes: the target-user wording in the vision and the descriptions in the specification, decision records and source comments. Existing decisions keep their dates, approval states, behavior and recorded evidence.

## Decision

Describe Unagi Reader through its own purpose, concrete behavior and quality requirements. Acceptance is judged against the specification's scenarios and budgets. The target user remains an engineer who reads many feeds every day. In older records, remove the design comparisons' product names while retaining the recorded requests, observations and verification limits. Do not add a new reason to a dated record. Historical observations are not new acceptance criteria or verification of the app.

## Not in this change

Runtime behavior, the product's value and promises, dependencies, assets or Git history. Names needed for licenses, platform guidance, development tools, past integrations and import verification stay factual. Unverified behavior remains unverified.
